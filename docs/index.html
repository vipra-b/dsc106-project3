Lightning Doesn't Light Most U.S. Wildfires

Folk belief blames lightning for wildfires. NOAA and the USDA say roughly 85% are human-caused. Here is what one season of GOES-16 satellite data (June–October 2024) shows about where lightning struck and where the country burned.

Draft notice. Fire pixels are real GOES-16 ABI-L2-FDCC detections (1 snapshot/day, 20:00 UTC, binned to 0.5° cells). Lightning is mock data with realistic CONUS climatology while the real GLM extract finishes.

1. Where America burned in 2024

Each circle = a 0.5° cell that registered fire activity during the selected week. Size = total radiative power (MW). Color = peak intensity. Drag the slider to scrub through the season.

2. Where lightning happens vs. where fires happen

Each state is colored on two dimensions at once — how much it burned and how much lightning hit it during the 2024 fire season. Click any state to open a detailed breakdown.
